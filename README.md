# ⚽ Building Elite Football Talent

An interactive **Tableau dashboard** designed to explore the key factors behind elite football talent, including player ratings, potential, age, market value, league performance, and technical skills.

The dashboard combines multiple visualizations into a single analytical view to help identify **high-performing players, top leagues, skill patterns, and player development opportunities**.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of football players through several key perspectives:

### Key Performance Indicators

* **Overall Rating:** 73.96 average
* **Potential Rating:** 76.35 average
* **Average Age:** 27.54 years
* **Development Gap:** 2.390

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall player population and their development potential.

---

## 📈 Visualizations

### 1. Market Value vs. Overall Rating

A scatter plot showing the relationship between:

* Player overall rating
* Market value
* Rating tier

Players are categorized into different rating tiers such as:

* Professional
* Good
* Excellent
* Elite
* World Class

This visualization helps identify whether higher-rated players tend to have higher market values and highlights exceptional players.

---

### 2. Top 10 Leagues by Player Rating

A horizontal bar chart ranking leagues based on their average player rating.

The visualization highlights leagues such as:

* Premier League
* La Liga
* Pro League
* Bundesliga
* Serie A
* Ligue 1
* Liga Portugal
* Eredivisie
* Super Lig
* Fortuna Liga

This allows users to quickly compare the quality of talent across major football leagues.

---

### 3. Player Rating by Age

A line chart showing how average player rating changes across different age groups for each rating tier.

The chart helps explore:

* How player performance changes with age
* Differences between rating tiers
* The age range associated with elite performance
* Potential performance trends throughout a player's career

---

### 4. Skills Associated With Higher-Rated Players

A heatmap comparing average technical and physical attributes across rating tiers.

The analyzed skills include:

* Defending
* Dribbling
* Pace
* Passing
* Shooting
* Physical

This visualization makes it easier to identify which skills tend to distinguish higher-rated players from lower-rated players.

---

## 🎯 Key Analytical Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

* Which leagues produce or contain the highest-rated players?
* How does market value change as player rating increases?
* At what ages do players typically reach their highest ratings?
* Which technical skills are strongest among elite players?
* How do skills differ between Professional, Good, Excellent, Elite, and World Class players?
* Which players combine high ratings with high market value?
* Where is there a potential gap between current overall rating and player potential?

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

* **Premier League** has the highest average player rating among the displayed leagues.
* Higher-rated players generally show substantially higher **market values**, although there are exceptions.
* **Elite and World Class players** demonstrate significantly stronger technical attributes compared with lower-rated tiers.
* **Dribbling, passing, pace, shooting, and physical ability** show strong differences across rating categories.
* The **potential rating exceeds the current overall rating**, indicating room for player development within the analyzed population.
* Player rating varies across age groups, with different rating tiers following distinct performance patterns.

## 🚀 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform football player data into an **interactive talent analytics solution**.

Instead of looking at individual player statistics separately, the dashboard brings together **performance, potential, age, market value, league, and skills** to provide a broader understanding of what contributes to elite football talent.
